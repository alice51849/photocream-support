Getting started

  • Shoot: the app opens to the camera. Pick a look from the strip — the viewfinder shows exactly what you’ll capture.
  • Edit a photo: tap the photo button (top‑left of the camera) to open one from your library straight into the editor.
  • Beauty: tap the face button to reveal the live Beauty slider for smoothing, glow and brighten.

Steal‑the‑Look

In the editor, tap Steal a Look and choose a photo whose colours you love. PhotoCream builds an editable colour recipe from it — all on device.

Frames, crop & pro tools

Open Adjust for the pro tools: Light, Colour, Effects, Beauty, Curve, Detail, Crop and Frames. Crop includes aspect presets and a straighten dial; Frames includes classic, studio and instant‑film borders.

Saving & sharing

Tap Save to render your photo, then Share to post straight to Instagram, TikTok, Messages and more, or save to your Photos library.

Watermark & unlock

Editing and previewing are free. Free exports include a small watermark; the one‑time unlock removes it and opens every premium pack and tool. It’s a single purchase — no subscription. Restore it any time from the unlock screen.

Photos don’t appear?

Check iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PhotoCream, and Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → PhotoCream.
