Questions and answers

What is PhotoCream Pro?

An iPhone and iPad film camera and photo editor by Lumi Studio. Its Steal the Look feature reads the colours of any photo you pick, on your device, and turns them into an editable film grade you can apply to your own shots. It also has 100 film looks with grain, halation, light leaks and vignette.

Who is it for?

People who like the look of film photos, want to recreate the colours of a photo they love on their own pictures, or want to see a look before they press the shutter. In the camera you see each look, its halation and light leaks live before you shoot; grain is added to the saved photo.

What editing tools does it have?

Curves, HSL and split toning; a face-only Beauty slider in the camera that smooths skin while eyes stay sharp; eye enlarging and natural body lengthening; text, frames, grain, and a date stamp that uses the photo's own capture date. The premium Y2K pack adds 10 early-2000s digicam looks such as Flash, Cyber and Bubblegum.

How is it paid for?

It is free to download. The camera is unlimited for everyone, and Original shots always save without a watermark; a small watermark appears only on photos with looks or effects until you unlock. A one-time in-app unlock removes the watermark and opens every premium pack. No subscription and no ads.

How is it different from other filter apps?

Instead of only picking a fixed preset, you can pick any photo and turn its colours into your own editable film grade. Looks, halation and light leaks are previewed live in the camera before you shoot. Everything stays on your device and your photos are never uploaded. Paying is a single one-time unlock rather than a subscription.
